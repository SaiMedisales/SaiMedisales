About Sai Medisales

Sai Medisales, established in 1999, is a proprietorship concern based in Baramati, Pune, and is your dedicated partner in veterinary healthcare and nutrition. Under the proprietorship of **Mr. DILIP**, we have built a reputation for distributing high-quality veterinary medicines and animal feed supplements. Our commitment to animal well-being drives us to provide a comprehensive range of products, from essential vaccines and injections to nutritional bolus and feed supplements, serving the agricultural and dairy sectors with excellence and reliability.

Our mission is to support the health and productivity of livestock and poultry by supplying effective and scientifically-backed products. We believe that healthy animals lead to a thriving agricultural community. We are constantly expanding our product portfolio to meet the evolving needs of our customers.
